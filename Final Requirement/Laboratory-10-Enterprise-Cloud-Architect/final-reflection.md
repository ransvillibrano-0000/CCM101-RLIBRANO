# Final Reflection – Mission 10

## What We Learned

This project helped us understand how Ubuntu Server, Docker, Docker Compose, networking, firewall security, persistent storage, and automated backups work together in an enterprise cloud environment.

## Challenges and Solutions

Our main challenges were configuring the virtual machine, connecting the Docker containers, and integrating the custom WordPress website. We resolved these through testing, troubleshooting, and verification.

## Project Outcome

We completed a persistent WordPress and MySQL application with firewall protection and automated database backups. We tested the system to verify container operation, data persistence, security, and automation.

## AI Assistance

AI tools were used to assist us with troubleshooting, clarification, and documentation. The system configuration, testing, and final implementation were performed and verified by our group.
